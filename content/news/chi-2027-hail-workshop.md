We’re co-organizing **HAIL: Bridging AI, HCI, and Learning Sciences for Human–AI Interaction in Learning** at CHI 2027. The workshop brings together AI researchers, interaction designers, learning scientists, and educators to ask how teams across these fields can define problems together, agree on what counts as evidence, and involve educators and learners throughout a project.
