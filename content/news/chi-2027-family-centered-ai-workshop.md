We’re co-organizing **Growing Up (and Old) with AI: Co-Constructing the Future of Family-Centered AI in Care Ecosystems** at CHI 2027. Building on our CSCW 2026 workshop, we’ll explore how AI affects family relationships and caregiving across generations, including how families navigate AI use with teachers, clinicians, and other people who support them. The workshop will bring together perspectives from child–computer interaction, education, social computing, and healthcare to co-design future tools.
