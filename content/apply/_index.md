# Work with Us

The Extended Horizon Lab at NC State University studies how **Extended Reality (XR)** and **AI** can support how people learn, collaborate, connect, and stay in control of intelligent technologies. We are always looking for motivated students and collaborators to join us.

Our work spans three areas:

- **XR + AI Systems** — building immersive and intelligent systems
- **User Experiments** — studying how people actually use them
- **Data Analysis** — making sense of what we observe

## PhD Students

We recruit PhD students through the NC State Computer Science graduate admissions process. If you are interested in working with us:

1. Apply to the [PhD program in Computer Science](https://www.csc.ncsu.edu/academics/graduate/) at NC State
2. Mention the Extended Horizon Lab and Prof. Qiao (Georgie) Jin in your statement of purpose
3. Email Prof. Jin after submitting your application

**What we look for:**
- Background in computer science, HCI, learning sciences, or a related field
- Interest in XR, AI, and human-centered research
- Research experience is a plus, but not required
- Strong communication and collaboration skills

## Masters Students

Masters students interested in research should reach out with:
- A short description of the research directions that interest you
- Your CV and relevant coursework or project experience

We generally expect a commitment of at least two semesters.

## Undergraduate Students

We welcome motivated undergraduates who want research experience. Please send:
- Your resume/CV and unofficial transcript
- A brief note on which of our [projects](/projects/) interests you and why
- Your expected availability (hours per week and number of semesters)

## Visiting Researchers and Interns

We occasionally host visiting students and researchers. Please reach out directly with your CV, a description of your research and how it connects to ours, your proposed dates, and your funding situation.

## Contact

For questions about joining the lab, email Prof. Qiao (Georgie) Jin at [qjin4@ncsu.edu](mailto:qjin4@ncsu.edu).

We look forward to hearing from you!
