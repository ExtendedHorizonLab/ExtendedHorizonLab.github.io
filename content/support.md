**EH Lab** (listed on the App Store as "Extended Horizon Lab") is the members' app of the Extended Horizon Lab in the Department of Computer Science at North Carolina State University. It brings together:

- **Today:** today's lab events, quick actions and overdue devices
- **Schedule:** the lab's Google Calendar in month, week and day views, and, if you turn it on, your own meetings with the advisor
- **Equipment:** who has which device, and borrowing and returning in a few taps
- **EH LLM:** search the lab's document archive and ask questions about it

The app works in English and Simplified Chinese on iPhone and iPad.

## Who can use it

Signing in is for current members of the Extended Horizon Lab. You sign in with your NC State Google account, which must be on the lab's member list. Anyone can try the app without an account: tap **View demo** on the sign-in screen to explore it with sample data.

## How to get access

1. Ask a lab manager to add your NC State email address to the lab's member list.
2. Install the app and tap **Sign in with Google**.
3. Choose your NC State Google account and allow the permissions Google shows. Keep the permission to see events on public calendars selected: the app needs it for the lab calendar. It does not read your own calendar unless you turn that on in Settings.

There is no sign-up in the app. When you leave the lab, a lab manager removes you from the list.

## Frequently asked questions

### The app says my account isn't on the list.

Make sure you picked your NC State Google account, not a personal one. If you did, ask a lab manager to add you, then tap **Try a different account** and sign in again.

### Google showed a list of permissions and I unticked one. Now I can't get in.

The app needs the permission to see events on public calendars to show the lab calendar. Sign in again and keep that permission selected. Your own calendar is not read unless you turn that on in Settings.

### How do I see my own meetings with the advisor?

Open **Settings** (tap your picture on the Today screen) and turn on **Show my meetings with the advisor**. Google asks once for read-only access to your calendar. Only meetings that include the advisor are shown, only on your device, and they are never sent to the lab. Turn the switch off to stop.

### Borrowing failed and says someone else just borrowed the device.

Two people tried to borrow it at the same moment and the other request arrived first. Pull down to refresh the list to see who has it now.

### Is the equipment list the same as the lab's spreadsheet?

Yes. The app reads and writes the lab's existing equipment sheet. Changes made in the app show up in the sheet, and edits made in the sheet show up in the app after a refresh.

### EH LLM says the service is restarting or not reachable.

The lab's archive server may be restarting, which takes about a minute. Try again shortly. If it keeps happening, contact us with the reference code shown in the message, if there is one.

### What is sent to OpenAI when I use EH LLM?

**Find files** sends your question so it can be reworded for the search; **Ask** also sends the matching passages from the lab's archive so an answer can be written. The app asks for your permission first. Your own calendar is never sent. See the [privacy policy](/privacy-policy/).

### How do I change the language?

Open **Settings** and choose **Language**. A few items that the system draws itself switch after you quit and reopen the app.

### How do I sign out or remove the app's access?

Open **Settings** and tap **Sign out**. To remove the app's access to your Google account entirely, go to [Google Account → Third-party connections](https://myaccount.google.com/connections).

## 中文版 {#zh}

**EH Lab**（在 App Store 上的名称为 “Extended Horizon Lab”）是北卡罗来纳州立大学计算机科学系 Extended Horizon Lab 的成员专用 app，包括：

- **今天**：今天的实验室安排、快捷操作、逾期未还的设备
- **日程**：实验室的 Google 日历，可按月、周、日查看；打开设置后，还能显示你自己与导师的会议
- **设备**：哪台设备在谁手上，点几下就能借用、归还
- **EH LLM**：检索实验室的资料库，并就其中的内容提问

app 支持 iPhone 和 iPad，界面有简体中文和英文。

### 谁可以使用

只有 Extended Horizon Lab 当前的成员可以登录。登录使用你的 NC State Google 账号，这个账号必须在实验室的成员名单上。没有账号也可以先试用：在登录页点 **查看演示**，用示例数据浏览 app。

### 如何开通

1. 请实验室管理员把你的 NC State 邮箱加入实验室成员名单。
2. 安装 app，点 **使用 Google 登录**。
3. 选择你的 NC State Google 账号，并允许 Google 列出的权限。请保留“查看公开日历上的活动”这项权限，显示实验室日历需要它。除非你在设置里打开，app 不会读取你自己的日历。

app 内没有注册功能。离开实验室时，由实验室管理员把你从名单中移除。

### 常见问题

#### app 提示我的账号不在名单上。

请确认你选的是 NC State 的 Google 账号，而不是个人账号。如果没选错，请联系实验室管理员把你加进名单，然后点 **换一个账号** 重新登录。

#### Google 列出权限时我取消勾选了一项，现在进不去。

app 需要“查看公开日历上的活动”这项权限来显示实验室日历。请重新登录，并保留这一项的勾选。除非你在设置里打开，app 不会读取你自己的日历。

#### 怎样显示我自己与导师的会议？

打开 **设置**（在“今天”页面点你的头像），打开 **显示我与导师的会议**。Google 会请求一次只读访问你日历的权限。只显示有导师参加的会议，只显示在你的设备上，不会发送给实验室。关掉开关即可停止。

#### 借用失败，提示别人刚借走了这台设备。

两个人几乎同时借同一台设备，对方的请求先到。下拉刷新列表，就能看到现在在谁手上。

#### 设备列表和实验室的表格是同一份吗？

是的。app 直接读写实验室现有的设备表格。在 app 里的改动会出现在表格里，在表格里的修改刷新后也会出现在 app 里。

#### EH LLM 提示服务正在重启或无法连接。

实验室的资料库服务器可能在重启，大约需要一分钟，请稍后再试。如果一直这样，请联系我们，并附上提示里的参考编号（如果有）。

#### 使用 EH LLM 时，哪些内容会发送给 OpenAI？

**搜索文件** 会发送你的问题，用来改写成检索词；**提问** 还会发送资料库中匹配到的段落，用来写出回答。发送前 app 会先征得你的同意。你自己的日历绝不会被发送。详见 [隐私政策](/privacy-policy/#zh)。

#### 怎样切换语言？

打开 **设置**，在 **语言** 中选择。少数由系统绘制的内容，要在退出并重新打开 app 后才会切换。

#### 怎样退出登录或撤销 app 的权限？

打开 **设置**，点 **退出登录**。如果要彻底撤销本 app 对你 Google 账号的访问权限，请前往 [Google 账号 → 第三方关联](https://myaccount.google.com/connections)。
