**Last updated: September 22, 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how the Extended Horizon Lab application (the "App") handles your information. The App is operated by the Extended Horizon Lab in the Department of Computer Science at North Carolina State University.

The App is an internal tool used by lab members to manage lab personnel, schedules, and related lab information.

## Information We Collect

When you sign in with Google, the App receives only basic account information from your Google account:

- Your name
- Your email address
- Your Google account profile picture
- Your Google account identifier

We request only the `openid`, `email`, and `profile` scopes. The App does **not** request access to your Google Drive, Gmail, Calendar, contacts, or any other Google service.

## How We Use Your Information

Your basic account information is used only to:

- Sign you in to the App
- Identify you to other members of the lab within the App
- Associate your schedule and lab information with your account

We do not use your information for advertising, and we do not sell, rent, or trade it.

## How Your Information Is Stored

**Information obtained through Google Sign-In is stored locally on your own device.** It is not transmitted to or stored on our servers.

Because this information is held on your device, it is removed when you sign out of the App, clear the App's data, or uninstall the App.

## Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We do not share your information with third parties, except where required by law.

The App uses Google Sign-In for authentication. Your use of Google Sign-In is governed by [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## Google API Services Limited Use

The App's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements.

## Data Retention and Deletion

Because your information is stored locally on your device, you control it directly. You can remove it at any time by signing out of the App, clearing the App's data, or uninstalling the App.

You may also revoke the App's access to your Google account at any time from your [Google Account permissions page](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions).

## Children's Privacy

The App is intended for use by lab members and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

## Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information. Because account information remains on your own device, its security also depends on the security of your device and your Google account.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
