We’re always looking for self-motivated students to join the lab as PhD students or research interns. The Extended Horizon Lab studies how extended reality and human-centered AI can support learning, collaboration, and social connection. If you’re interested in working with us, take a look at our [publications](/publications/) to see what kinds of questions we ask.

## Prospective PhD students

The [Extended Horizon Lab PhD Student Guide](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1c3yT4UM0niOylnzBuLVnwC3JqKBMy-PCk_5J_QLPMKk/edit) describes how [Qiao (Georgie) Jin](/people/qiao-jin/) advises students and what she expects from them. Apply through the [NC State Computer Science PhD program](https://csc.ncsu.edu/academics/graduate/phd/) and explain in your statement why you want to work with Georgie and what you hope to study. After submitting your application, you’re welcome to email Georgie with a brief introduction and a description of your research interests. The department’s admissions committee reviews applications first.

## NC State undergraduate and master’s students

Email Georgie with your résumé or CV, a brief note on the research that interests you, and how much time you can commit during the semester. A relevant course project or work sample is welcome. Lab members attend the weekly group meeting and meet regularly with their project mentor.

## Postdocs and visitors

If you’re interested in visiting or collaborating, email Georgie with your CV, the research you’d like to pursue together, and your proposed dates. Please mention any funding you already have or plan to apply for.

## Getting started

We value collaboration and put research integrity ahead of deadlines and publication goals. Once you’ve arranged to join the lab, read the [EH Lab Student Guide for Onboarding](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1LP4L_kMLXbFan7jz7vB5MZGY4kxt579xiW56WWfQiHg/edit) for expectations and first steps. PhD students should read both guides.
