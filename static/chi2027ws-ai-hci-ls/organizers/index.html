Organizers

HAIL Workshop @ CHI 2027

Our organizing team spans AI, HCI, learning sciences, computing education, learning analytics, and educational practice. Collectively, the organizers have developed and studied AI-supported learning systems, worked with educators in authentic settings, and led workshops, conferences, and research communities across disciplinary boundaries. The team itself reflects the cross-community perspective that the workshop seeks to foster, bringing together expertise in technical development, interaction design, learning research, and educational practice.
