Workshop Schedule

9:00 – 9:20
Welcome & Workshop Framing
Overview of goals, themes, and expected outcomes; participant introductions.
9:20 – 10:20
Interactive Poster Session
Participants present accepted and work-in-progress papers using laptops, printouts, or organizer-supported printing.
10:20 – 10:30Transition / Screen break
10:30 – 11:00Coffee break
11:00 – 12:15
Activity 1: From Family-Centered Theory to Defining Family-Centered AI
Card sorting, affinity diagramming, and discussion tables examining core questions about defining family, centering values, and translating theory into practice.
12:15 – 12:30Group photo and transition
12:30 – 2:00Lunch

Activity 2: How AI Shapes Intergenerational Dynamics
Structured roundtable discussions on Growing Up with AI, Growing Old with AI, and the broader institutional ecosystem. Participants rotate across tables.
3:00 – 3:30Coffee break
3:30 – 4:30
Activity 3: Futures for Family-Centered AI
Human-AI collaborative co-design using generative AI tools and provocative prompt cards. Groups produce speculative design images or future scenario documents.
4:30 – 5:00
Synthesis & Closing Discussion
Full-group synthesis of common themes, open questions, shared conceptual dimensions, and next steps for the research agenda.
