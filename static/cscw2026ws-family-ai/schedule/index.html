Workshop Schedule

Full-day program at CSCW 2026

9:00 – 9:20
Welcome & Workshop Framing
Overview of goals, themes, and expected outcomes; participant introductions.
9:20 – 10:20
Interactive Poster Session
Participants present accepted and work-in-progress papers using laptops, printouts, or organizer-supported printing. Informal exchange to establish common ground.
10:20 – 10:30 Transition / Screen break
10:30 – 11:00 Coffee break
11:00 – 12:15
Activity 1: From Family-Centered Theory to Defining Family-Centered AI
Organizers introduce family-centered design theories (Family Systems Theory, Ecological Systems Theory, Family Resilience). Participants engage in card sorting and affinity diagramming to group AI technologies, then form discussion tables to examine core questions: Who is included in "the family"? Which relationships, roles, and forms of care are centered? What values should family-centered AI protect? Each group documents its work on a shared artifact. Concludes with a 10-minute debrief.
12:15 – 12:30 Group photo and transition
12:30 – 2:00 Lunch
2:00 – 3:00
Activity 2: How AI Shapes Intergenerational Dynamics
Structured roundtable discussions on three topics: Growing Up with AI, Growing Old with AI, and the broader institutional ecosystem around family AI. Each table has a facilitator and note-taker identifying: (1) what AI systems are entering family settings, (2) how these systems change relationships and care practices, (3) emerging tensions or risks, and (4) groups that may be excluded or differently affected. Participants may rotate across tables. Concludes with a summary of cross-cutting issues.
3:00 – 3:30 Coffee break
3:30 – 4:30
Activity 3: Futures for Family-Centered AI
Human-AI collaborative co-design using generative AI tools and provocative prompt cards. Example prompts: "If you were drafting the first 'Family AI Protection Act,' what guardrails would you mandate?" or "If you were designing an embodied AI caregiver for a multi-generational home, how would it navigate conflicting commands?" Groups produce a speculative design image or future scenario document.
4:30 – 5:00
Synthesis & Closing Discussion
Full-group synthesis generating: (1) shared conceptual dimensions for defining family-centered AI, (2) summary of AI's impact on family life, and (3) initial research and design agenda. Discussion of next steps, future collaborations, and open questions.

Growing Up (and Old) with AI: Co-Constructing the Future for Family-Centered AI
