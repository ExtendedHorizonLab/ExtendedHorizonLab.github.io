Call for Papers

NextGen Learning Interfaces Workshop @ AIED 2026

Overview

We invite submissions from researchers and practitioners working at the intersection of AI, HCI, and the learning sciences.

Submissions may include research papers, position papers, system descriptions, design frameworks, or work in progress. We particularly encourage submissions that bridge disciplines and explore novel approaches to designing, evaluating, and deploying AI-supported learning interfaces.

Topics of Interest

AI-Powered Learning Systems and Interfaces

  • Designing AI-powered learning interfaces and tools
  • Developing teacher-facing and learner-facing AI systems
  • Creating multimodal, embodied, and immersive AI-enabled learning environments

Human-AI Interaction and Learning Experience

  • Human-AI interaction and collaboration in educational settings
  • Supporting feedback, scaffolding, and reflection in AI-supported learning
  • Enabling collaborative and social learning with AI

Evaluation and Real-World Deployment of AI in Education

  • Evaluating AI-supported learning systems in authentic educational contexts
  • Bridging AI, HCI, and learning sciences in real-world educational settings

Challenges, Trade-offs, and Open Problems

  • Transparency, interpretability, and user control in AI-supported learning
  • Challenges in integrating AI, HCI, and learning sciences in practice
  • Trade-offs between AI capability, usability, and pedagogical effectiveness

Submission Types

Full Workshop Paper

6–8 pages

Research papers, position papers, system descriptions, design frameworks, or work in progress.

Demo Submission

3–4 pages + video (≤ 1 min)

System or tool demonstrations with a short accompanying video.

Formatting

All submissions must be in Springer format. Papers that do not use the required format may be rejected without review.

Authors should consult Springer's authors' guidelines and use their proceedings templates (LaTeX or Word).

Submissions must follow Springer policies on publication, including policies on the use of AI in the authoring process.

Review Process

All submissions will be reviewed by the workshop organizers for relevance, quality, and potential to stimulate interdisciplinary discussion. Accepted submissions will be invited for presentation during the workshop.

Accepted submissions will be listed and promoted on the workshop website. We encourage authors to share their work on arXiv or other open-access repositories to increase visibility and impact.
