EXTENDED HORIZON LAB

We are a research lab led by Professor Qiao (Georgie) Jin in the Department of Computer Science at North Carolina State University.

We build and study human-centered AI and immersive systems to understand and support how people learn, collaborate, connect, and exercise control over intelligent technologies. Our research spans:

  • Systems: Mixed reality and AI systems and interfaces that support learning, collaboration, and social connection
  • Experiments: User studies of how people learn, collaborate, and exercise control when interacting with AI and immersive technologies
  • Analysis: Educational data mining and learning analytics to understand learning processes, evaluate outcomes, and inform system design

To learn more, see our recent projects and publications.
