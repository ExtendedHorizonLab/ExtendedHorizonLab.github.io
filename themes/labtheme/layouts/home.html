Extended Horizon Lab

We build and study interactive systems that bring together human-centered AI and extended reality (XR). We envision intelligent spaces that connect the physical and digital worlds, respond to people’s needs, and help them learn and collaborate while respecting their values, rights, and boundaries.

  • AI as an enabling technology for XR

    We build social and learning agents that interpret speech, gestures, and other cues in context so they can respond to people in ways that fit the activity.

  • XR as a method for investigating AI

    We use immersive settings to study how people learn with and about AI, including how hands-on experiences can help them understand how AI works and recognize its biases.

  • XR + AI ecosystems

    We design tools that make it easier for people with different levels of technical experience to create immersive content, share knowledge, and collaborate. We study the safety, security, and ethical questions that arise as people use these tools.

The Extended Horizon Lab is led by Qiao (Georgie) Jin in NC State’s Department of Computer Science.
